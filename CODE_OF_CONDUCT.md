# Code of Conduct

ClocDot contributors and maintainers are expected to provide a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone.

Examples of acceptable behavior include constructive feedback, respect for
different experiences and viewpoints, accountability, and focusing on what is
best for the community. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, publication of private information, and sustained personal attacks
are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate. Reports should be
made privately through the repository's security-advisory channel and will be
reviewed as confidentially and impartially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
